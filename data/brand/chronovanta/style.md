# CHRONOVANTA – Official YouTube Thumbnail Style Guide

Identity: Future Science Editorial + Scientific Blueprints + Cinematic Technology.
Futuristic, but not generic glowing robots or excessive neon graphics.
Must look different from Abyssoryn, Quasorynth and DimeHorizon.

Core style: clean, intelligent, premium, scientific, slightly futuristic.

## Palette
- #111318 Graphite (background)
- #F4F2ED Ivory (headline)
- #FF6A00 Orange (key word, year, scientific annotations)
- #8E959C Steel
- #D9E4E8 Ice Gray

## Typography
- Main headline: Space Grotesk Bold, 85–110 px
- Large numbers (2050, 2100): Anton, 125–165 px
- Scientific labels: IBM Plex Mono Medium, 22–30 px
- Supporting headline: Sora ExtraBold, 42–60 px
- Small brand mark: Space Grotesk Bold, 22–28 px

## Composition
- Long video, 1280 × 720: bold headline on the left 45%, one dominant photorealistic future scene on the right 55%, orange scientific accents.
- Short, 1080 × 1920: brand on top, headline in the middle, the scene below.
- 2–5 headline words. One word, number or year highlighted in orange. A large year only when the video is really about it.
- Small CHRONOVANTA monogram in a corner, never louder than the headline.

## Visual details
Thin orange schematic lines, futuristic technical labels, restrained measurement graphics, scientific diagrams only where relevant.
Future technology must read as a conceptual reconstruction, never as a confirmed invention.

## Avoid
Generic neon-blue sci-fi graphics, excessive glowing robots, fantasy spaceships, cartoon visuals, cluttered backgrounds, exaggerated effects, random scientific symbols, distorted faces, incorrect lettering, unrealistic technology claims, misleading predictions.
