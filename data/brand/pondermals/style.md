Long videos (1280x720), mystery + science + storytelling: cream/yellow with navy contrast; the animal large on the left or right; headline 3-5 words, e.g. "DO CATS LOVE US?"; one clear scientific mystery or surprising animal expression; consistent Pondermals logo.
Headlines: all capitals, a curious question, never claim more than the video does.
